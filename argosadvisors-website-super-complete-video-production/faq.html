FAQ

Answers before a production gets scoped.

What does Argos Advisors produce?+

Brand films, commercials, product video, interviews, customer stories, events, livestreams, podcasts, social content and post-production.

Do you handle strategy and scripting?+

Yes. Strategy, concepts, scripts, shot planning and pre-production can be scoped before filming.

Can you work from existing footage?+

Yes. Archive re-edit and post-only engagements can turn existing footage into new deliverables.

Do you create platform-specific versions?+

Yes. Deliverables can include vertical, square, widescreen, short-form and captioned versions when included in scope.

Can you travel for productions?+

Location production can be scoped based on crew, travel, permits, insurance and local production requirements.

Are the prices on this prototype final?+

No. Pricing shown here is demo/sample pricing. Final production quotes depend on scope, crew, locations, talent, equipment, usage and post-production needs.

How does a project start?+

Usually with a production brief or discovery call covering audience, goals, channels, timeline, deliverables and budget range.

Do you offer recurring content support?+

Yes. Monthly and quarterly content programs can be structured for brands that need an ongoing production rhythm.
